Deposit Funds

Add cash to your wallet. Deposited funds can be used to buy stocks, bonds, and mutual funds; selling or redeeming a holding credits the wallet back.

About Your Wallet

Your wallet funds every buy transaction across Stocks, Bonds, and Mutual Funds, and is credited whenever you sell a holding or redeem a matured bond. Buys are rejected if the wallet balance is insufficient. View the full deposit/buy/sell ledger in the Transactions tab.
